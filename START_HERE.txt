NIGHT SHIFT / PRODUCTION CONTROL
PASS TWO · VERSION 0.2

START
Extract the entire ZIP into a permanent folder. Open index.html in a modern
browser. All files must stay together. No installation, account or network
connection is required.

UPGRADING FROM PASS ONE
1. Open your old app and click Backup. Keep the downloaded JSON.
2. Extract this version into its own folder and open index.html.
3. Click Restore and choose the pass-one JSON. Review the preview and confirm.
4. The app adds new tasks/records while preserving your existing notes, people,
   custom quests, dates and progress. Expanded readiness gates that were Done
   are flagged for review. New audit tasks start open.
5. Download a fresh backup from this version. Do not restore it into pass one.
If you replace the app files at their original path, browser storage may be
retained, but a backup is still necessary. Local file storage is browser-dependent.

PASS TWO FEATURES
107 initial quests: 80 master-plan tasks, a dedicated DP quest, 20 audit tasks,
and six date-linked shooting-readiness quests.
Every quest has an editable intro, definition of done, notes, deadline,
checklist and dependencies. Selected complex original quests have new checklists.
Completed tasks from pass one are not silently cleared to add these checklists.

Shoot days: proposed dates, location/access, scene assignment, productive-hour
capacity, additional reserve, people, role, call time, and date-specific availability.
Day editing can reassign scenes from another day; the old day is also reopened
for review. Moving a date resets availability/access and shifts linked quest dates.
You can leave dates unknown. Nothing invents availability or confirmed bookings.

Scene register: all 14 scenes, cast, time estimates, requirements, shoot assignment
and footage status. Telescope exteriors intentionally have no shoot day.

Production Book: people, locations, equipment, props and VFX sequence records.
Props/VFX link to scenes using IDs S1-S14. Add individual shot records as coverage
becomes concrete; seeded VFX records are sequence-level placeholders.

Quest links: a shoot-day-relative deadline, an optional full-day readiness gate,
a required location/equipment, and reference links to scenes and budget lines.
Required links affect completion. Reference links do not assert completion.

Budget: cash versus in-kind; separate estimated, committed and actual values.
Blank = unknown, not zero. Exposure takes the largest value for each cash line,
so commitment and spend are not counted twice. A EUR 1,500 illustrative envelope
is seeded; fees, venue/equipment and accommodation remain unpriced.

Timeline: live quest targets, shoot days, equipment collection/return dates,
and dated prop/VFX records, plus a section for unscheduled quests/days.
Enter asset dates as YYYY-MM-DD. Open-work and all-work views are available.
A warning appears if a prerequisite's target is later than its dependent quest.

DEADLINE COLOURS
Relative to your computer's local date:
Green: more than 14 days. Yellow: 8-14. Orange: 3-7. Red: 2 or fewer, including
past-due work. Text labels explain every colour. Completed/excluded tasks are
neutral. Status (blocked/waiting/review) remains separate from urgency.
Colours refresh after changes/navigation and at the next local day when idle.

READINESS
Director, DoP and Sound are core role checks. Use those exact labels in a day's
comma-separated role field. Add the scene's character names for cast assignments.
Other crew roles are supported; quest X20 covers the full responsibility audit.
A confirmed day needs dated availability, role commitments, call times, location,
access, assigned scenes, sufficient productive hours and confirmed equipment.
Conflicts are flagged if a person is assigned to two active days on the same date.
Set-hour estimates exclude meals, general setup, moves and wrap. The AD must still
review actual working hours, logistics and setup time. This is a planning aid.

SAVING
Forms commit when you press Save. The top bar reports browser-save status.
Keep regular portable JSON backups, especially before moving folders or upgrading.
Use one tab at a time. Detected external changes pause editing rather than merge.
Undo covers the last 30 changes in the current session. Restore validates data,
asks for confirmation and downloads a recovery copy before replacing the project.
Check that downloads actually complete. Backups contain entered contact details.

AUDIT ADDITIONS
Academic deliverables, asset/music/participant permissions, AI documentation,
location emergency arrangements, costume comfort, proven backup recovery,
crew expense terms, dietary/eating needs, continuity, room noise, essential
coverage, overnight storage, returns/expenses, credits, subtitles, submission
playback, presentation preparation, release/excerpt approval and crew ownership.
These expose decisions to confirm; they do not claim legal or institutional approval.

VALIDATION
Logic tests passed for seed integrity, dependency cycles, schema-1 migration,
retained user data, linked deadline shifts, the day-edit handler, availability
reset, readiness checks, capacity, record links, unknown dates, budget math,
backup roundtrip, every view/editor and the asset timeline.
Browser layout and native browser file/download interactions remain unverified
in this build environment. Do a small save/reload/backup/restore trial locally
before entering substantial new information.
