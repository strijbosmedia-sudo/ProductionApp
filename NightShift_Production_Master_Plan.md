### 11. Daily production procedure

Before call: rooms and kit ready, attendance/transport confirmed, food and water available, today's essential coverage visible. At briefing: AD establishes scene order, cast readiness, sound issues, effect plates and any door/floor/desk action. Tim directs; coordinator handles incoming logistics.

For each setup: rehearse blocking, check frame and continuity, record slate/sound, note preferred take, capture scheduled plate or wild line, then release the setup. For running/door/floor action, agree movement and clear the path before takes. Desk-height alien staging requires a suitable supported arrangement, not an untested office desk.

Before leaving each room: room tone, clean plate where needed, missing inserts, continuity stills, readable screen shots and prop states. At wrap: verified copies, media log, next-day needs, charging, clean-up and keyholder handover. Schedule copying time and storage transport explicitly; Tim should not inherit an undefined overnight data shift after directing.

### 12. Editing, sound, colour and handoff plan

Choose acquisition frame rate, resolution, colour settings, audio settings, naming and sync method before October's workflow test. Preserve originals and metadata. Use proxies if they materially improve editing. One project naming/version convention applies across edit and VFX; do not overwrite an issued turnover.

Suggested media structure: camera originals by day/card; sound originals by day/card; sync/proxies; projects; VFX by shot ID; graphics; music/VO; references; exports; documentation. Three independent copies, with one kept separately from the active working setup when possible. Validate recovery from a sample before production.

Sound handoff: locked reference picture with visible timecode and version, agreed AAF or other tested exchange format, original production audio, dialogue/music/effects separation, agreed handles, script, VO and cue/notes list. Confirm the collaborator can import a test before committing. Temporary sound is not a missing sound plan: dialogue intelligibility, atmosphere, alien voice and major narrative effects must work in the examination version.

Grade handoff: locked reference, tested XML/AAF/EDL or flattened-master workflow as agreed, original media or explicitly agreed intermediates, VFX finals, colour-management notes, LUTs if used, reference stills and list of intentional looks. Keep temporary grades removable. Have the grader confirm relink and framing before the final delivery rush.

If shots change after turnover, send a versioned change list and replacement materials; do not silently send a new picture with an old audio timeline.
